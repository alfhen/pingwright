Shared hashes

Sends GraphQL persisted-query requests with a made-up query ID, and loads a script whose name holds a content hash. The hash of an email that is not the run identity is sent where a skip rule could wrongly hide it: ?leak=email in the query of a tracking request, ?leak=nested as variables.customer.id of the persisted-query POST, ?leak=pixel in the name of a u-<hash>.gif pixel.
